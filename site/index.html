2035 ARCHITECTURE · DESIGN PHASE

One foundation. Two generations of vehicles.

Designed for OEMs, Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers, silicon and embedded AI developers. Support standalone edge, companion, zone and integrated real-time placements. Every component has an explicit contract; every deployment unit has a replacement policy; allocated system objects are discoverable through authorized views.

  • Native Zephyr, no compulsory proprietary core tools or AI runtime.
  • Static composition by default; selected multi-image or isolated-module updates.
  • Measured, simulated, stale and unavailable data remain distinct.
  • Customer-specific configuration and evidence without mandatory core forks.

Physical target, supplier and qualification claims require exact configuration evidence.
